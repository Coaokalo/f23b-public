F-23B

Name: F-23B Black Widow II
Type: Experimental community aircraft
Crew: 1

Requirements:
DCS World 2.9.29.27468 on Windows
Installed and activated DCS: F/A-18C Hornet

Cockpit and systems:
The pilot uses the installed F/A-18C Hornet cockpit and avionics.
The external aircraft contains cockpit geometry from the licensed YF-23 model.
Flight forces are provided by the F-23B experimental flight bridge.
Native systems and sound use the installed Hornet.

Armament:
Internal gun
AIM-9X Block II on the two outward-swinging bay doors
Three AIM-424 MALICE or AIM-120C missiles in the main bay
Quick Start missions carry two AIM-9X Block II and three MALICE missiles.

Installation:
Both F-23B module folders and the native missile installer are required.
The installer modifies AIM-120B and AIM-9X globally and includes rollback.
See the download's INSTALL.md for the complete instructions.

Limitations:
Single-player experimental preview. Full-envelope flight, VR, and multiplayer
remain unqualified. Block II post-launch datalink/LOAL is not implemented.
Performance is a simulator design approximation, not measured YF-23 flight data.
